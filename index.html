What defines Jimmie Ward's role in professional football?

Jimmie Ward is a professional American football safety, primarily known for his versatility in the defensive secondary. He has played both free safety and strong safety, demonstrating adaptability across multiple NFL teams including the San Francisco 49ers, Houston Texans, and Arizona Cardinals.

Ward primarily plays as a safety, a defensive back position responsible for covering deep passes and assisting in run support. He has also played cornerback during his career, showcasing his ability to cover various offensive assignments. Safeties are integral components of NFL defenses.

Jimmie Ward began his NFL career after being selected 30th overall in the 2014 NFL Draft by the San Francisco 49ers, where he played for nine seasons.
